**AutoQuant**, our agentic research system, takes work from hypothesis to capital. Candidates are proposed and tested day and night, run in a live mirror before any capital is committed, and only a small fraction of what is proposed is kept. Each cycle starts from everything the last one established.

Three ideas from our research notes shape how it is built:

- **Research capacity, not ideas, is the binding constraint.** Most desks are not short of hypotheses. They are short of the capacity to examine them.
- **What changes when research never stops.** Continuity turns research from a project into a standing capability.
- **On retiring a system that still looks profitable.** Decay is expected. The decision to stop should not wait for proof.
